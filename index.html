Our Training Formats

We offer structured sessions tailored to developing sound grappling foundations and technical proficiency.

Gi Classes

Traditional Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu training wearing the kimono. Focuses heavily on grip fighting, leverage, and positional control.

Gi Training

No-Gi Classes

Fast-paced grappling wearing rashguards and shorts. Emphasizes wrestling transitions, leg locks, and modern submission holds.

No-Gi Training

Open Mat

Dedicated sparring time for practitioners to refine techniques, drill positions, and roll freely with training partners.

Open Mat

Coaching & Training Community

Training at Raijin is built on mutual respect, technical development, and safety. Our coaches provide detailed, methodical instruction designed to help every student progress at their own pace.

Our community includes local residents as well as international visitors from around the globe, creating a diverse and engaging training atmosphere.
