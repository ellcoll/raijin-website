Drop-In Training for Visitors

Travelling through Hanoi? We welcome visiting BJJ practitioners from academies worldwide to drop in, train, and share knowledge on our mats.

Planning Your Visit

We love hosting visiting grapplers from all affiliations. To ensure a smooth drop-in experience while respecting our members and coaching schedule, please review our visitor guidelines.

Hanoi is a vibrant destination, and training while travelling is one of the best ways to experience the local martial arts community.

1. Check in With Us

You're always welcome to train at Raijin! We recommend sending us a quick message to let us know if you plan to drop in so we can confirm class times for you.

Message Us on Messenger

2. Equipment & Gear

For no gi, please bring your own clean attire. For gi classes, it's great if you have your own, but we also have gis you can borrow with a 50.000vnd laundry fee.

3. Drop-in Fees

Drop-in sessions are 200.000vnd, we're able to accept cash payments and bank transfers to a Vietnamese account. We aren't currently able to accept Apple Pay payments.

If you're planning on staying a while, consider our monthly membership.
