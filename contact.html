Directions & Arrival

Finding the Gym: The easiest way to find us is from Âu Cơ Road. Near Nhật Tân Elementary School (the large, colourful building), turn into Alley 374 Âu Cơ. Follow the alley for approximately 200 metres. Raijin BJJ is on your right.

Transport: Ride-hailing apps like Grab are widely available across Hanoi and provide direct transport to our location. Please confirm the address as simply searching for Raijin on Grab could lead to our old (nearby) location.

Arrival Tip: We recommend arriving a couple of minutes before your scheduled class time to change, wrap tape if needed, and meet the instructor.
